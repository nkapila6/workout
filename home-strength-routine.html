Full body · dumbbells only

The repeat routine

Same five moves every session. No deciding, no planning. You show up and repeat. That is the whole point.

2–3×PER WEEK
~25MINUTES
3×8–12SETS × REPS
Warm-up 3–4 min · no weight

Gets blood into the joints and rehearses the moves before you load them. Skipping it at your weight is how a back gets tweaked on deadlifts.

  • 20 arm circles + 10 shoulder rolls
  • 10 bodyweight squats slow, full depth
  • 10 hip hinges the RDL motion, no dumbbells

Then make the first set of each lift a lighter feeler set. Don’t static-stretch cold, it does nothing useful before lifting.

Do these in order, rest 60–90 sec between sets

01

Goblet squat

Legs
3 sets × 8–12 reps

Hold one dumbbell at your chest. Sit down between your knees, chest up, heels flat.

02

Dumbbell floor press

Chest · push
3 sets × 8–12 reps

Lie on your back, dumbbells at chest. Press straight up until arms lock, lower until elbows touch floor.

03

One-arm dumbbell row

Back · pull
3 sets × 8–12 each side

Hinge over, one hand braced on a bench or thigh. Pull the weight to your hip, squeeze, lower slow.

04

Romanian deadlift

Hamstrings · glutes
3 sets × 8–12 reps

Soft knees, push hips back, slide weights down your legs. Feel the stretch, drive hips forward to stand.

05

Shoulder press

Shoulders
3 sets × 8–12 reps

Dumbbells at shoulder height. Press straight overhead until arms lock, lower under control.

How to not quit

1

Start at 2× a week. A lower bar means you actually show up. Add the third session once two feels automatic.

2

Cap it at 30 minutes. Long workouts are why people quit. Short and repeatable beats perfect and abandoned.

3

Add reps first, then weight. When all 3 sets hit 12 clean reps, bump the dumbbell up. That slow climb is the whole game.

4

Stack it on a habit. Same days, same time, right before or after something you already do. Music or a podcast on.

5

Enjoyment comes after progress. The first 3–4 weeks feel like a chore for everyone. Push through and the numbers going up get addictive.

Keep your 10k walks as your cardio. This is the muscle side. Works with fixed or adjustable dumbbells.
